I'm glad I held onto my CD booklet.

I have a son now, and I want him to be able to flip through physical media, see the album art.

I connected in new ways to my mom by digging through the vinyls I dug out of the closet. I learned more about the how and why of my dad's brain by scanning the spines of his library of books.

I want my son to experience something more sense-driven and personal than thumbing through fucking Apple Music.
