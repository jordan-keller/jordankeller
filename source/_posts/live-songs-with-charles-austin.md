I joined a band 3 months before I left Chicago. My buddy, Charles, asked me if I'd be down to learn and record live performances of four songs with us and his two friends, Joe Odarisio ([The Metalliques](https://themetalliques.bandcamp.com/)) and James Burke.

Of course, I get to join a fun band with great musicians and even better dudes that practices 5 minutes from where I live 3 months before I move out of the city.

Two of the songs ("Lollicops" and "Pastoral Scene") were written by Brock Winthrop aka Alex Nichols (E1, Chapo). One, "Let's Start a War" is written by Charles Austin (E1, Solipse), whose latest release, "Containers" you can hear [here](https://charles-austin.bandcamp.com/album/containers). The other song is a cover by the band Asian Kung-Fu Generation.

I have a hard time picking my favorite from this bunch: the brave political anthem ("Let's Start a War")? The deconstructed Sabbath of "Lollicops"? The hypermelodic janglepop of "Pastoral Scene"? Or the melodic joint-fitting of Asian Kung-Fu Generation?

Had a blast learning and playing music in a meat space.
